PACIO Announcements

2025

  • September 8, 2025

    The PACIO Advance Healthcare Directives Interoperability (ADI) Implementation Guide passed the 2025 HL7 September ballot and is currently in ballot reconciliation.

  • August 4, 2025

    The PACIO Advance Healthcare Directives Interoperability (ADI) Implementation Guide (STU2-ballot) submitted for September 2025 HL7 Ballot.

  • July 23, 2025

    The PACIO Personal Functioning and Engagement implementation guide published by HL7 as STU-2 (Standard for Trial Use 2).

  • July 16, 2025

    The PACIO Project successfully tests and demonstrates how the Transitions of Care (TOC) IG and the Standardized Medication Profile (SMP) IG can be used to exchange a patient's transitions of care information necessary by many post-acute care disciplines. Medication reconciliation and structured FHIR action plan was also demonstrated using the data exchanged using the SMP IG.

  • July 16, 2025

    The PACIO Project successfully tests and demonstrates how the Advance Healthcare Directives (ADI) Implementation Guide can be used to exchange structured Personal Advance Care Plans (PACPs), structured Portable Medical Orders (PMOs), and minimally structured Mental Health Advance Directives (MHAD) (new).

  • May 16, 2025

    The PACIO Standardized Medication Profile (SMP) Implementation Guide published by HL7 as STU-1 (Standard for Trial Use 1).

  • May 6, 2025

    The PACIO Transitions of Care (TOC) Implementation Guide (STU1-ballot) passes May 2025 HL7 Ballot.

  • March 28, 2025

    The PACIO Transitions of Care (TOC) Implementation Guide (STU1-ballot) submitted for May 2025 HL7 Ballot.

  • January 15, 2025

    The PACIO Project successfully tests and demonstrates 12 PACIO and other FHIR Implementation Guides working together as a system of systems in the PACIO Integration Track, showing the art of what is possible in a realistic, complex healthcare scenario. The scenario included 12 different system roles and 100 system transactions, incorporating post-acute care assessments, advance healthcare directives, medication lists, medication reconciliation, cancer, prior authorization, directory services, care plan management, and care team management. A video of the demonstration is available.

2024

  • September 22, 2024

    The PACIO Project successfully tests and demonstrates how the Standardized Medication Profile (SMP) IG and the Transitions of Care Implementation Guide can be used to exchange a patient's transitions of care information necessary by many post-acute care disciplines. Medication reconciliation was also demonstrated using the data exchanged using the SMP IG. A video of the demonstration is available.

    September 22, 2024

    The PACIO Project successfully tests and demonstrates how the Advance Healthcare Directives (ADI) Implementation Guide can be used to exchange structured Personal Advance Care Plans (PACPs), structured Portable Medical Orders (PMOs) (new), and unstructured Mental Health Advance Directives (MHAD).

    September 17, 2024

    The PACIO Personal Functioning & Engagement Implementation Guide (STU2-ballot) passes the HL7 September 2024 ballot.

    August 13, 2024

    The PACIO Personal Functioning & Engagement Implementation Guide (STU2-ballot) submitted to HL7 September 2024 ballot.

    July 18, 2024

    The PACIO Project successfully tests and demonstrates how the Standardized Medication Profile (SMP) IG and the Transitions of Care Implementation Guide can be used to exchange a patient's transitions of care information necessary by many post-acute care disciplines. Medication reconciliation was also demonstrated using the data exchanged using the SMP IG using a more complex use case and integrating more patient medical record data. A video of the demonstration is available.

    July 18, 2024

    The PACIO Project successfully tests and demonstrates how the Advance Healthcare Directives (ADI) Implementation Guide can be used to exchange structured Personal Advance Care Plans (PACPs), unstructured Portable Medical Orders (PMOs), and unstructured Mental Health Advance Directives (MHAD). The participants also tested a better way to revoke an advance healthcare directive.

    May 19, 2024

    The PACIO Project successfully tests and demonstrates how the Standardized Medication Profile (SMP) IG and an early draft of the Transitions of Care Implementation Guide can be used to exchange a patient's transitions of care information necessary by many post-acute care disciplines.

  • May 19, 2024

    The PACIO Project successfully tests and demonstrates how the Advance Healthcare Directives (ADI) Implementation Guide can be used to exchange structured Personal Advance Care Plans (PACPs), unstructured Portable Medical Orders (PMOs), and unstructured Mental Health Advance Directives (MHAD). A video of the demonstration is available.

  • February 29, 2024

    The PACIO Project successfully tests and demonstrates how the Personal Functioning and Engagement (PFE), Physical Activity, and Gravity Social Determinants of Health Clinical Care (SDOH-CC) Implementation Guides can be used to enable data relevant to post-acute care during the PACIO Testing Event. The testing follows the patient through their care journey through a tightly-coupled use case and be available for decision making by the patient and their caregivers and family. A video of the demonstration is available.

  • January 11, 2024

    The PACIO Advance Healthcare Directive Interoperability (ADI) implementation guide was published as STU-1 (Standard for Trial Use 1). View the published ADI implementation guide here: PACIO Advance Healthcare Directive Interoperability Implementation Guide 1.0.0 - STU1

  • January 5, 2024

    The PACIO Personal Functioning and Engagement (PFE) implementation guide was published as STU-1 (Standard for Trial Use 1). View the published PFE implementation guide here: PACIO Personal Functioning and Engagement Implementation Guide 1.0.0 - STU1
